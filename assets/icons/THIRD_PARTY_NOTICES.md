- [Devicon](https://github.com/devicons/devicon) — React, Next.js, Node.js, Netlify, Cloudflare, PostgreSQL, GitHub Actions, Visual Studio Code, Figma, Python, Claude, npm and Google Cloud. MIT license: https://github.com/devicons/devicon/blob/master/LICENSE.
- [Simple Icons](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons) — GSAP mark. CC0-1.0: https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/develop/LICENSE.md.

Logos and wordmarks are trademarks of their respective holders. Tile framing is the original visual treatment of this profile repository. Inclusion means a tool is present in the owner's development, design or learning workflow, not proficiency, certification or endorsement.

- [Lobe Icons](https://github.com/lobehub/lobe-icons) — OpenAI/ChatGPT logo mark used to identify ChatGPT as a tool in this workflow. MIT-licensed icon distribution: https://github.com/lobehub/lobe-icons/blob/master/LICENSE. ChatGPT and its logo are OpenAI trademarks; see https://openai.com/brand/ for usage terms. No endorsement or affiliation is implied.

## Additions for the 37-icon profile grid

New tiles follow the existing dark rounded tile design and are locally hosted. The following repositories supplied icon artwork:

- [Devicon](https://github.com/devicons/devicon) (MIT): [WebGL](https://github.com/devicons/devicon/blob/master/icons/webgl/webgl-original.svg), [Kali Linux](https://github.com/devicons/devicon/blob/master/icons/kalilinux/kalilinux-original.svg), [Canva](https://github.com/devicons/devicon/blob/master/icons/canva/canva-original.svg), and the [Google G mark](https://github.com/devicons/devicon/blob/master/icons/google/google-original.svg) used inside the Google Business Profile tile.

## Brand mark audit — 41-tool profile

The existing 34 sourced marks were inspected and retained with their documented Devicon, Simple Icons and Lobe Icons provenance. These are third-party distributions of brand artwork, not a guarantee that every asset is the latest owner-issued variant. WebGL retains its sourced geometry with a light monochrome fill for contrast.

Four former letter placeholders have been replaced with sourced SVG artwork:

- [Three.js](https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/blob/dev/files/icon.svg) — upstream project icon; original black mark on a light backing.
- [esbuild](https://github.com/evanw/esbuild/blob/main/images/logo.svg) — upstream project logo; original colors.
- [WebXR](https://github.com/immersive-web/webxr-samples/blob/main/media/logo/webxr-logo-mark.svg) — mark published by the Immersive Web working group.
- [MediaPipe](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/develop/icons/mediapipe.svg) — Simple Icons brand geometry; [Google's published icon reference](https://developers.google.com/static/mediapipe/images/mediapipe_icon.svg). The owner-hosted SVG could not be downloaded during this update; the local mark is a monochrome library variant.

The following three entries remain in the same positions as neutral name-only tiles, with no invented marks:

- [axe-core](https://github.com/dequelabs/axe-core) — [Deque's axe family repository](https://github.com/dequelabs/axe) references a logo, but a verified local logo file could not be retrieved.
- [Instinct](https://instinct.com/) — no verified downloadable brand asset obtained.
- [Google Business Profile](https://www.google.com/business/) — no current verified downloadable mark obtained; the former Google My Business logo is not used as a substitute.

All 41 tools remain listed; all images are repository-hosted. Brand names and marks belong to their respective owners; no endorsement, affiliation or certification is implied.
